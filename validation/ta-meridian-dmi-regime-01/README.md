# PineForge Meridian DMI Regime

An independently authored PineForge directional-movement strategy. It consumes
the three-value `ta.dmi` result, applies an EMA regime filter, and keeps
execution to fixed-size market entry/close with an ATR stop.

This candidate is private and unqualified until its exact TradingView export
and native PineForge Corpus comparison both complete with an Excellent result.

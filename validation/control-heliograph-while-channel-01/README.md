# PineForge Heliograph While Channel

An independently authored PineForge breakout strategy whose recency-weighted
centerline is calculated with a bounded `while` loop. Dispersion defines the
entry band; fixed-size market orders, a centerline exit, a holding boundary,
and an ATR stop keep the example coherent.

This candidate is private and unqualified until its exact TradingView export
and native PineForge Corpus comparison both complete with an Excellent result.

# PineForge Tranche Partial Market Close

An independently authored PineForge staged-exit strategy. Its first objective
closes half of a four-contract position through a market close, leaving the
remainder under the ordinary trend and risk rules.

The Apache-2.0 source is PineForge-owned. Admission requires the exact private
TradingView trade export and an Excellent result from the native PineForge
Corpus verifier; deterministic compilation alone is insufficient.
